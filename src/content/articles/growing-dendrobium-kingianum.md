Dendrobium kingianum is a hardy, compact species native to Australia, and
one of the most forgiving orchids you can grow. It tolerates a wide
temperature range, blooms reliably once mature, and produces keikis
readily — making it an easy plant to both grow and propagate.

## Light and temperature

Bright, indirect light suits this species well. It also tolerates cooler
winter temperatures better than many orchids, and in fact benefits from a
noticeable day-night temperature swing to trigger blooming.

## Watering

Allow the growing medium to dry out between waterings. Overwatering is the
most common cause of problems with this species — err on the side of less.
