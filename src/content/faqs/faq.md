## How do I care for my orchid after it arrives?

Every genus has slightly different needs, so the best place to start is our
[Growing Instructions](/astro-ecommerce/growing-instructions/) page — it covers
light, watering, and temperature guidance by type.

## What is the easiest kind of orchid to grow?

*Dendrobium kingianum*, a native of eastern Australia, is an exceptionally hardy orchid to grow.  In its native range, temperatures drop to around freezing during the winter, and can hit triple digits during the summer.  It also goes through a dry spell during winter where it does not receive any rain, making this an excellent orchid for those looking to get growing experience with a bullet-proof orchid!  This orchid has nearly everything:
- handles extremes of temperatures
- tolerant of drought/water deprivation -- it gets this naturally in Australia during winter months!
- **Extremely** fragrant blooms
- Makes keikis/propagations very easily
